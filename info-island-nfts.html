PAGE 2 · THE SURPRISE

A GIFT FROM A FRIEND!

One sunny morning, Pip the frog found a message: “A special digital collectible is waiting for you!” Pip did a happy hop. “Let's ask a grown-up to help!”

PAGE 3 · GET READY

MEET PHANTOM WALLET

Phantom is like a little treasure backpack for digital collectibles on Solana. With a grown-up, Pip opens the official Phantom app or visits phantom.com to get started.

PAGE 4 · FIND THE GIFT

FOLLOW THE TRUSTED LINK

Pip opens the gift link shared by the person who sent it. A grown-up checks that the website address is the real one. Then they find the CONNECT WALLET button.

PAGE 5 · CONNECT

SAY HELLO TO PHANTOM

Tap CONNECT WALLET, choose Phantom, and check the request inside the Phantom app. Only connect if the website is the trusted gift page. Pip's grown-up checks the wallet address is the right one.

PAGE 6 · CLAIM

OPEN THE GIFT!

If the page has a CLAIM NFT button, Pip's grown-up reads the Phantom transaction carefully. They check what it will do and whether a small Solana network fee is shown. If anything looks strange or Phantom shows a warning, they stop. If it all matches the expected gift, the grown-up can approve it.

PAGE 7 · TREASURE FOUND

LOOK WHAT YOU GOT!

Hooray! When the claim finishes, open Phantom and look in the Collectibles area. It may take a little while to show up. If it is missing, ask a grown-up to check the transaction and the Solana wallet address.

PAGE 8 · FROG-SAFE RULES

THE THREE BIG SAFETY RULES

1. Never share your Secret Recovery Phrase or private key. 2. Never type those secret words into a website. 3. Always ask a grown-up to check links and transactions. If someone asks for secret words or tells you to hurry, STOP and get help.

1 / 8
GROWN-UP CHECKPOINT!
This story is for learning, not financial advice. A child should never set up or use a crypto wallet alone. An adult must verify the gift website and every transaction. A real gift should never require sharing a recovery phrase. Some claims may show a network fee; check it before approving.
