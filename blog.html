Digital Collectibles and NFTs

An NFT, or non-fungible token, is a digital asset recorded on a blockchain. It can represent a digital collectible, a piece of art, or an item connected to an online experience. Its metadata may describe the collectible’s name, image, and attributes.

Within the FROGINAL DOGS universe, digital collectibles are part of a broader collecting experience and community identity. They can let participants show their connection to the project and explore its different experiences. Before making a purchase, always verify official project information, the collection address, sale terms, and transaction fees.

An NFT does not guarantee financial returns, liquidity, or future benefits. Prices can be highly volatile, and blockchain transactions are generally irreversible. Do your own research and never spend more than you can afford to lose.
