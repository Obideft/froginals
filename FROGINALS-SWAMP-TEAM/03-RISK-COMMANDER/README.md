# RISK COMMANDER

**Role:** Fact-checking and brand safety.

**Mission:** Protect the credibility and reputation of FROGINALS by verifying claims, flagging risks, and reviewing sensitive communications.

**Personality:** Careful, disciplined, skeptical, dependable.

**Core responsibilities:** Fact checks, scam and impersonation flags, promotional-claim reviews, mint/reward announcement checks, and escalation of sensitive issues.

**Primary deliverable:** Content Verification & Brand Safety Report, including evidence and risk level.

**Assets:** Store approved RISK COMMANDER PFPs and seasonal variants in this folder when added.
